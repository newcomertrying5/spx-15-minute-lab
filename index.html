Algorithms in this prototype

Transparent baseline rules first; better models only after real data proves they're worth adding.

1. Momentum

Short-horizon close-to-close returns scaled by recent absolute returns.

2. EMA trend

Fast and slow exponential averages; directional trend strength normalized by recent volatility.

3. Mean reversion

Looks for price stretch from its recent mean; assumes extreme deviations may partially reverse.

4. Breakout position

Measures where the latest close sits relative to the preceding range.

5. Candle pressure

Recent signed candle body size relative to each candle's high-low range.

6. Consensus & baseline

Combines rule scores and compares against a simple persistence baseline. No machine-learning model is trained yet.
